Sir / Madam,

Under Section 6(1) of the Right to Information Act, 2005, I request the
following information:

1. Machine-readable (CSV / JSON / spreadsheet) copies of the **final
   electoral rolls** published after the Special Intensive Revision (SIR) for
   the States/UTs where SIR has been completed, including but not limited to
   Bihar.

2. Machine-readable copies of the **electoral rolls as they stood immediately
   before the SIR** (the pre-SIR rolls used as the baseline for the revision),
   for the same States/UTs.

3. If machine-readable copies are not held, the **complete list of URLs** of
   the officially published roll PDFs (district / Assembly Constituency /
   part-wise) for the pre-SIR and post-SIR rolls referred to above.

4. The **record-retention / archival policy** under which pre-revision
   electoral rolls are preserved and made available to the public.

The electoral roll is a public document under Section 22 of the Registration
of Electors Rules, 1960, and copies are sold to the public on payment of fee;
I seek the same public information in machine-readable form to enable
independent verification of the revision exercise. I am not seeking any
information exempted under Sections 8 or 9 of the Act.

## Filing notes

- File one RTI to the **ECI** (covers the central roll database question) and,
  if the reply deflects to states, file follow-ups to the respective **Chief
  Electoral Officers** (Bihar, UP, West Bengal first).
- If the CPIO denies machine-readable copies citing format, the fallback ask
  (point 3, the URL list) is independently valuable: it converts the
  CAPTCHA-gated portal into a citable public inventory.
- Track the 30-day clock; first appeal goes to the First Appellate Authority,
  ECI, if there is no reply.
- Keep every reply: a denial in writing is itself evidence for the
  methodology page ("rolls requested, not provided in machine-readable form").
